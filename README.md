# clipfinder

Finds the best moments of a Twitch VOD from its chat, its sound and your markers, then
cuts and exports them for TikTok, Shorts or DaVinci Resolve.

This repository only hosts the Windows app's downloads.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/f2ire/clipfinder-releases/releases/latest/download/clipfinder.zip)** (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, ~500 MB)

Beta: Premium (your OBS mic in the ranking, exports without a limit) is free while it lasts.

The app bundles third-party programs and models (FFmpeg under the GPL v3, whisper.cpp,
SenseVoice, TwitchDownloader...): their licenses and sources are listed in
`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`, inside the zip.
